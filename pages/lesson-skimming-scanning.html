Lesson Plan

Skimming and Scanning

Academic Reading · Intermediate English Language Learners · Approximately 65 minutes

Lesson Information

Course
Academic Reading
Learner Level
Intermediate English Language Learners
Class Length
Approximately 65 minutes
Topic
Skimming and Scanning
Reading Focus
Reading efficiently to locate main ideas and specific information

Learning Objectives

By the end of the lesson, students will be able to:

  1. Explain the difference between skimming and scanning.
  2. Skim a text to identify its topic, main idea, organization, and overall purpose.
  3. Scan a text to locate specific facts, names, dates, numbers, and keywords.
  4. Choose an appropriate reading strategy based on the purpose of a reading task.
  5. reflect on how skimming and scanning can improve reading speed and comprehension.
