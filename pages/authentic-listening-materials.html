Teaching Materials

Authentic Listening Materials

Original dialogues and listening tasks based on real-world communication contexts.

As part of a curriculum development initiative at the BYU English Language Center, I designed and developed a series of authentic listening materials to provide multilingual learners with meaningful exposure to spoken English in real-world contexts. Rather than relying solely on commercially published resources, I created original dialogues and listening tasks that reflected situations students are likely to encounter in everyday life, including healthcare appointments, grocery shopping, workplace communication, and academic interactions.

Each resource was developed with specific pedagogical objectives in mind. Dialogues were carefully written to incorporate target grammatical structures, functional language, and high-frequency vocabulary while maintaining natural conversational flow. To support listening instruction, I produced accompanying audio recordings, learner transcripts, and instructional materials that could be adapted for intensive listening, dictation, transcription, pronunciation practice, and grammar-focused activities. Several materials also included intentionally inaccurate versions alongside corrected transcripts, allowing students to develop noticing skills through error detection and language analysis.

This project strengthened my ability to design authentic instructional materials that balance linguistic accuracy, communicative authenticity, and pedagogical effectiveness. It also reinforced my interest in creating curriculum resources that prepare learners for successful communication beyond the classroom by connecting language instruction with real-world experiences.
