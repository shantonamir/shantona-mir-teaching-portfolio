Overview

This project combined large-scale vocabulary assessment development with a mixed-methods research study investigating the use of generative AI in vocabulary test creation. The project resulted in the development of approximately 3,000 AI-assisted vocabulary assessment items while examining the efficiency, quality, user experience, and training needs associated with AI-assisted item generation.

  • Conducted quality assurance through iterative editing and peer review.
  • Refined AI prompting strategies to improve item quality and consistency.

Research Design

Methodology

  • Convergent Parallel Mixed Methods
  • Online Surveys
  • Semi-Structured Interviews
  • Quantitative Statistical Analysis
  • Qualitative Thematic Analysis

Research Questions

  • How efficient is AI-assisted vocabulary test generation?
  • What quality issues occur most frequently in AI-generated assessment items?
  • How do users perceive AI-assisted assessment development?
  • What training and support are needed for effective AI integration?

Key Findings

  • AI significantly improved development efficiency while requiring continued human oversight.
  • Prompt quality strongly influenced both efficiency and item quality.
  • Human revision remained essential for producing pedagogically appropriate assessment items.
  • Participants demonstrated increased confidence and competence following structured AI use and collaborative training.
