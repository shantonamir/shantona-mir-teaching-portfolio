Research Profile

Citation Format

Preferred APA 7th and MLA citation formats.

Please cite my publications using APA 7th edition unless otherwise specified by the publisher or venue. Where available, each publication includes a DOI, publisher link, or open-access version to support accurate citation and easy access.

Preferred Citation

Mir, S. (Year). Title of the work. Journal Name, Volume(Issue), pages. https://doi.org/xxxxx

Example :

APA 7th Format ( Note: 0.5 inch hanging indentation required)

Mir, S., & Roy, S. (2024). Assignment again! Investigating the psychological perspectives of assignment and assignment deadlines among tertiary-level students. English Language Teaching Perspectives, 9(1–2), 117–130. https://doi.org/10.3126/eltp.v9i1-2.68725

MLA : Mir, Shantona, and Sukanto Roy. “Assignment Again! Investigating the Psychological Perspectives of Assignment and Assignment Deadlines Among Tertiary Level Students”. English Language Teaching Perspectives, vol. 9, no. 1-2, Aug. 2024, pp. 117-30, doi:10.3126/eltp.v9i1-2.68725.
